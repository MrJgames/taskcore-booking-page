How TaskCore can help

Repairs start with the problem you can observe. An item that feels loose, no longer moves as expected or needs replacement may call for a different approach than routine upkeep. Sharing those details helps TaskCore understand the task before discussing next steps.

  • Common household items that need repair or adjustment.
  • Replacing worn household components when the task and replacement are suitable.
  • A list of everyday upkeep items around a home or supported property.

What to share when you contact us

Make a short list of the affected items and note which problem matters most. Describe what changed, when you noticed it and whether a previous adjustment helped. If you already have a replacement part, include its name or model so the fit can be discussed before an appointment.

You can prepare a service request on the homepage or contact TaskCore directly. The form prepares a message on your device; you choose whether to send it by text or email.

Repair or new installation?

If your goal is to put together or mount something new, start with Installations & Assembly. If a device is physically in place but will not connect or respond, the home-technology services may be the better starting point.
