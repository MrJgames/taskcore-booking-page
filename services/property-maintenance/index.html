Property Maintenance & Inspections

A clear record of what needs attention while you are away.

TaskCore supports homeowners and property managers with property checkups and inspections, pre-arrival and departure visits, and practical punch-list support across the Coachella Valley. For vacation rentals and managed properties, an agreed checklist gives each visit a clear purpose and gives the owner a useful record of what was observed.

Property checkups and inspections

Agree on the rooms, accessible areas and household items to check before the visit. TaskCore can document visible conditions, note reported problems and identify items for follow-up. These are practical property checkups within the approved scope; they do not replace a specialized inspection or a licensed professional's assessment.

Pre-arrival and departure visits

Before an owner or guest arrives, share the arrival date and the items that need attention, such as a loose handle, an entertainment setup question or a short maintenance list. For departure visits, identify the areas to review and the condition notes you want returned. Confirm timing and access arrangements before booking; a requested date is not a confirmed appointment.

Punch lists and minor maintenance

Group small tasks by room and mark priorities. Minor maintenance is performed only within the approved scope, with replacements and additional work discussed before proceeding. For doors, household hardware and other everyday fixes, see Repairs & Maintenance. For device setup, see Smart Home & Home Technology.

Photo documentation and closeout notes

Specify which conditions or completed items need photos. Closeout notes can distinguish what was checked, what was completed and what still needs approval or another provider. For a managed property, identify the person who should receive the findings so the next step is clear. Avoid including guest information in the task list.

Vendor access and follow-up coordination

Vendor access or follow-up coordination can be arranged as part of the request. Share the vendor contact, appointment window, approved access procedure and the information you want documented. Coordination does not expand TaskCore's repair scope or replace the vendor's responsibility for its work.

Prepare a property visit

Include the community, property type, occupancy or arrival timing, prioritized checklist and the person authorized to approve work. Discuss secure access directly; do not put gate codes or passwords in a public message. For network issues noticed during a visit, review Wi-Fi & Connectivity.

Local help across the Coachella Valley

TaskCore serves Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella and Bermuda Dunes, California. Tell us your community and the task you have in mind when discussing an appointment.
