Help getting household items put together and in place.

TaskCore provides household installation, mounting, assembly and setup support in the Coachella Valley. From fixtures and hardware to items that arrive in parts, the starting point is understanding what you have, where it belongs and what needs to be ready for it.

How TaskCore can help

Assembly and installation are different parts of a job. Assembly brings the supplied pieces together; installation or mounting depends on the item, its instructions and the location. Tell TaskCore whether you need one step or both so the request can be discussed clearly.

  • Assembly and setup of household items supplied in parts.
  • Household fixtures and hardware that need installation.
  • Mounting and placement support for suitable household items.

What to share when you contact us

Share the product name or model, a link to its instructions if available, and the intended location. Note whether it is still boxed, partly assembled or replacing something already in place. Mention any missing parts you have noticed. These details help clarify the scope without guessing from a product photo alone.

You can prepare a service request on the homepage or contact TaskCore directly. The form prepares a message on your device; you choose whether to send it by text or email.

Does the item also need technology setup?

A mounted TV may still need streaming and soundbar setup. A connected-home device may need help joining your network. Use the related pages to describe both the physical task and the setup you need.
