Pool Controls & Diagnostics

Understand the controller problem before deciding on the next repair.

TaskCore offers Pentair pool-control diagnostics and Jandy AquaLink diagnostics focused on the controller interface, basic setup and troubleshooting. Share what the display or app reports, which schedule is affected and when the behavior changed. The goal is to document the control issue and clarify an appropriate next step, not to promise that every equipment problem can be fixed through a setting.

Displays, schedules and reported errors

TaskCore can review accessible controller displays, time and schedule settings, reported errors and the behavior you describe. Provide the controller model and a photo of the message if available. A displayed error is a starting point for investigation, not proof that a particular pump, heater or other component needs replacement.

Connectivity and basic setup

For an app that cannot reach the controller, describe whether the local display still works and whether the home network recently changed. Basic setup and troubleshooting depend on the installed model and authorized account access. Review Wi-Fi & Connectivity for broader router or coverage issues, and Smart Home & Home Technology for other connected devices. Keep passwords out of your initial request.

What this service does not include

TaskCore is not offering pool cleaning, chemical balancing, gas work, internal pump or heater repairs, or licensed electrical work through this service. Do not open equipment enclosures or attempt wiring changes to prepare for a visit. Work requiring a licensed professional is referred to an appropriate provider.

Documented findings and referrals

Findings can record the reported symptom, visible messages, settings reviewed, approved setup changes and unresolved issues. If the next step requires licensed work or equipment repair outside this service, TaskCore documents that limitation and recommends referral rather than proceeding beyond the agreed scope.

Request a controller diagnostic visit

Send the property community, controller brand and model, exact error text, affected schedules and whether the problem appears on the controller, app or both. For an unoccupied or managed home, Property Maintenance & Inspections can help frame separately arranged access and follow-up needs. Appointment scope and access must be confirmed before work begins.
