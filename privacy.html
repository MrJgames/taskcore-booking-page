TASKCORE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This notice explains how TaskCore handles information connected with the public website at taskcorepros.com. It covers information you choose to provide, the site’s optional Google Analytics measurement, and the choices available to you.

Information TaskCore may receive

Information you provide

You may choose to provide information when you call, text, email, prepare a service-request message, or book an appointment. Depending on how you contact TaskCore, that information may include your name, phone number, email address, service address, appointment details, the type of service requested, and the description or other details you include.

The service-request form on this website prepares a message in your browser. It does not submit the form to a TaskCore database. Information is sent only if you choose an available Text or Email action, or otherwise contact TaskCore directly.

Website usage information

If you accept Analytics, Google Analytics 4 may collect general usage information such as page visits and interactions, session statistics, referring source, approximate location, browser and device type, operating system, and similar technical information. TaskCore does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, service addresses, request descriptions, or other directly identifying contact information to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics and your choices

TaskCore uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general website traffic and improve the site. Analytics-related storage defaults to denied before you make a choice. The site also defaults advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization to denied.

If you select Accept, analytics storage is granted so Google Analytics can measure website usage. If you select Decline, analytics storage remains denied. Advertising-related consent remains denied under the site’s current configuration.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser. You can reopen the consent controls at any time using the control on this page or in the website footer. Clearing browser storage may cause the banner to appear again.

How information may be used

TaskCore may use information to:

  • respond to questions and service inquiries;
  • schedule, provide, and follow up on requested services;
  • communicate about appointments or work;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website;
  • understand general website traffic when Analytics consent is granted; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations or protect TaskCore, customers, and others.

Third-party services

The website uses or links to third-party services, including Google Analytics, Google Calendar appointment scheduling, website hosting, and the phone, text, or email services you choose to use. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Following a booking, email, text, or other external link takes you to or uses a service that TaskCore does not control.

Information retention

TaskCore may retain inquiries, appointment details, service communications, and business records for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain appropriate records, resolve issues, and meet legal or operational requirements. Google Analytics information is retained according to the settings and practices of the applicable Analytics account and Google. This notice does not promise a fixed retention period.

Your Analytics preference remains in your browser until you change it, clear the browser’s storage, or the preference is otherwise removed by the browser.

Security

TaskCore uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the website and the information involved. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

California privacy requests

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, California residents may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, or information about the use of their personal information. TaskCore has not represented that every provision of the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to this business.

You may contact TaskCore with a privacy question or request. TaskCore will review the request and respond as required by applicable law. TaskCore may need enough information to understand the request and reasonably verify that it concerns you.

Changes to this notice

TaskCore may update this Privacy Policy when website practices or services change. The date at the top will show when the notice was last updated.
